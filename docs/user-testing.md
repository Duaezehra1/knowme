# KnowMe: asking real parents and teachers to try it

## WhatsApp message (copy and send)

> Assalam o Alaikum! I'm a student building **KnowMe** for a Google hackathon. It's a free app for children who can't easily explain themselves (for example autistic or non-speaking children).
>
> Parents write down how their child communicates, what calms them and what upsets them. Then a teacher, babysitter or nurse can tap "Crying" or "Hurt" and instantly see what the parent said to do. It also makes a QR card for the child's bag, in case they get lost.
>
> Everything stays on the phone, nothing is uploaded.
>
> Could you try it for 10 minutes and tell me honestly what you think? It's here: https://knowme-passport.web.app
> (You can use the made-up example child "Sam" if you don't want to type real details.)
>
> Your feedback would really help me. JazakAllah!

## Three questions to ask afterwards

1. Would this have helped you (or a carer, teacher or nurse) with a child you know? When exactly?
2. What was confusing or missing?
3. Can I quote one sentence from you in my project (first name and role only, e.g. "Ayesha, special-needs teacher")?

## Who to ask

- Parents of children with autism, Down syndrome or speech delay (school or family WhatsApp groups)
- Teachers at a special-needs school or an inclusive school
- Speech therapists or occupational therapists
- Anyone who babysits or cares for a child with special needs

Write down their answers word for word. Two or three real quotes in the Devpost write-up and the demo video make the "Potential impact" score much stronger.
